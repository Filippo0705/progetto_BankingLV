Lista endpoint:

1) POST /accounts/{id}/deposita
2) POST /accounts/{id}/preleva
3) GET /accounts/{id}/movimenti
4) GET /accounts/{id}/movimenti/{n}
5) GET /accounts/{id}/saldo

Esempio chiamate

1) curl -X POST http://localhost:8080/accounts/1/deposita -d {"amount": "10000", "description": "Tangente"} -H "Content-Type: application/json"

2) curl -X POST http://localhost:8080/accounts/2/preleva -d {"amount": "-5000", "description": "Esselunga"}-H "Content-Type: application/json"

3) curl http://localhost:8080/accounts/2/movimenti

4) curl http://localhost:8080/accounts/1/movimenti/1

5) curl http://localhost:8080/accounts/1/saldo
